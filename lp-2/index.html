How do I know if my chimney needs cleaning?+

Visible soot, strong odors, debris, smoke problems or uncertainty about the last service are reasons to schedule an evaluation or cleaning. These signs can have different causes, so describe what you notice when booking.

Can creosote buildup be removed?+

Creosote varies in type and severity. The appropriate cleaning method depends on the condition and accessibility of the buildup. An evaluation helps determine what cleaning or further attention is needed.

Do I need to know exactly what's wrong before calling?+

No. Tell us what you can see, smell or notice when using your fireplace. You don't need to diagnose the problem yourself.

Do you clean fireplaces too?+

Yes. Fireplace cleaning is part of Santa Chimney Sweep's service offering. Tell us about your fireplace type and the areas you want cleaned when scheduling.

What if my chimney needs more than cleaning?+

Visible concerns or damage can be discussed separately. Cleaning is not a substitute for evaluating a damaged chimney or a fireplace with smoke or venting problems.

Can I book chimney cleaning online?+

Yes. Use Book Service Online to request service through our booking page, or call (512) 937-0590.

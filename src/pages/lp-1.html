How do I know if my chimney needs repair?+

Cracked bricks, missing mortar, water stains, loose components or changes in how your fireplace drafts can be reasons to have the chimney evaluated. Tell us what you can see and when it happens. An assessment helps determine the next step.

Can you repair a leaking chimney?+

Chimney leak repair is one of our services. The repair depends on where the water is entering, which may involve the crown, flashing, masonry, cap or chase cover. Finding the source comes before choosing a repair.

Can damaged bricks and mortar be repaired?+

Damaged masonry may need brick replacement, mortar repair or more extensive work. The appropriate approach depends on the condition and extent of the damage, so it should be evaluated first.

What causes chimney crown or flashing problems?+

The crown protects the top of the chimney, while flashing seals the roof junction. Weather exposure, cracks, deterioration or damaged seals can allow water in. An evaluation can help identify which area needs attention.

Can a damaged chimney liner be repaired or replaced?+

Liner or flue damage may call for repair, relining or replacement. The options depend on the liner material and its condition. Have it evaluated before deciding which repair is appropriate.

Do I need to know exactly what is wrong before calling?+

No. Describe the leak, damage, smell or other change you are noticing. Photos and details about when the problem happens can help explain your concern, but you do not need to diagnose it before contacting us.
